### One-line description

We help growing businesses address wasted ad spend and unclear campaign performance by delivering AI-assisted PPC optimisation, enabling higher ROAS and lower cost per acquisition.

### Service summary

An analyst-led, AI-assisted optimisation service for Google Ads accounts. We audit tracking and campaign structure, apply AI models to bidding and budget allocation, run structured experiments, and report on ROAS and CPA — so spend goes to what converts. Paid media spend is billed by the client directly and excluded from our fees.

### What this service is

- Ongoing or sprint-based optimisation of existing Google Ads accounts
- Measurement, tracking, and experimentation to improve efficiency

### What this service is not

- A one-off campaign build with no measurement follow-through
- Management of paid media budget on the client's behalf

### Primary problems addressed

**Common symptoms**

- Spend rising while conversions stay flat
- No clear view of ROAS or CPA by campaign

**Likely root causes**

- Incomplete conversion tracking
- Unstructured campaigns and manual bidding

**Business consequences**

- Wasted budget and missed revenue

### Intended outcomes

**Operational outcomes**

- Reliable conversion tracking and reporting

**Commercial outcomes**

- Improved ROAS and lower CPA

**Strategic outcomes**

- A repeatable experimentation system

### Primary deliverables

- PPC optimisation plan
- Campaign performance report
- Experiment roadmap

### Typical pricing model

Sprint-based engagement or monthly retainer. Pricing depends on account size, number of campaigns, tracking maturity, and reporting needs. Paid media spend is excluded.

### Typical timeline

Optimisation sprint 2-4 weeks; ongoing monthly optimisation thereafter.

### Related services

- **Prerequisite services:** Conversion tracking audit (or included in discovery)
- **Complementary services:** Web design / CRO
- **Common follow-on services:** AI engineering (custom automation)
