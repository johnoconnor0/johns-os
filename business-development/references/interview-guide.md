# Interview guide

The interview is a **mandatory gate** — never write an outline before completing it. Use
`AskUserQuestion`, batch related questions, and pre-fill from the service-type profile and
any ingested source. Ask only what is missing.

## Always confirm (addendum triggers)

- **Technical/Security/Compliance (module 8):** "Does this service handle personal or
  sensitive data, or integrate with the client's systems or hosting?"
- **AI Service (module 9):** "Does this service build or operate any AI/LLM systems?"

## Question bank by module

**1. Service Overview**
- What is the service, in one sentence? Who is the target customer and what is the core problem?
- What are the primary deliverables and the typical timeline?

**2. Customer Fit and Qualification**
- What business size, industry, and maturity is the best fit? What disqualifies a client?
- What events typically trigger a client to buy this? Who are the buying stakeholders?

**3. Scope and Deliverables**
- What is included vs explicitly excluded? What are the optional add-ons?
- What are the acceptance criteria and the definition of done?

**4. Delivery Plan**
- Which delivery model applies? What are the phases, milestones, and approval gates?
- What is the review-round and approval-turnaround expectation?

**5. Roles, Responsibilities, and Client Inputs**
- What must the client provide before and during delivery? What access is required, and by when?
- Who approves scope, design, and final acceptance?

**6. Success Measurement and Reporting**
- How is success measured (metrics, baselines, targets)? What is the reporting cadence and format?
- What attribution limitations should be stated up front?

**7. Risks, Dependencies, and Constraints**
- What are the top risks and mitigations? What external dependencies and constraints apply?

**8. Technical/Security/Compliance (if triggered)**
- What platforms, integrations, and environments are involved? Who owns hosting?
- What data types are handled, where is it stored, and what compliance applies?

**9. AI Service (if triggered)**
- What is the AI use case, model approach, and grounding? What human oversight is required?
- How is it evaluated and monitored? What are the data-use terms?

**10. Support, Warranty, and Handover**
- What is handed over, and how is training delivered? What is the warranty period and what does it cover?
- What ongoing support model, severity levels, and maintenance ownership apply?

## Quick mode

If the user asks to move fast, still confirm the essentials: service name, service type,
target customer, core problem, primary deliverables, and both addendum triggers.
