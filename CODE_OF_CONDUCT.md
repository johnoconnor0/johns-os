# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, focus on evidence, and keep technical disagreement constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and useful.
